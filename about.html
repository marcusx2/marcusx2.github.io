About

I'm an AR/VR developer with over 6 years of experience building immersive applications with Unity, working across multiple XR frameworks such as the Oculus Interaction SDK, AR Foundation, and Niantic Lightship.

Beyond immersive tech, I've stayed active on the web side throughout my career. The Unity plugins I publish here are a direct product of that crossover: bridging browser capabilities with Unity Web builds in ways that would otherwise require complex native integrations.

I also freelance on Upwork, where I've built a track record across AR/VR projects for clients around the world. I love learning, I love teaching, and I'm always looking for the next interesting problem to solve.
